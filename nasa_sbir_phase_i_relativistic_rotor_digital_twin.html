Dynamic Strain De-Aliasing & Relativistic Optical Metrology

Mathematical formulation for decoupling micro-strain mechanical runout, launch vibrational loading, and relativistic time dilation in spaceborne optical clocks.

1. Coupled High-G Phase Observable

$$\frac{\Delta f_{\text{total}}(t)}{f_0} = \underbrace{\frac{\omega^2 (R_2^2 - R_1^2)}{2c^2}}_{\text{Kinematic Time Dilation}} + \underbrace{\xi_{\text{PE}} \cdot \varepsilon_{rr}(t)}_{\text{Centrifugal Photoelastic Strain}} + \underbrace{\frac{\delta R_{\text{runout}}(t)}{R_0}}_{\text{Bearing Micro-Runout}}$$

2. Photoelastic Tensor & Cavity Elasticity

The mechanical strain tensor $\varepsilon_{ij}$ induced by centrifugal acceleration $\mathbf{a}_c = \omega^2 r \hat{\mathbf{r}}$ perturbs the optical path length and the refractive index ellipsoid through the Pockels photoelastic coefficients $p_{ijkl}$:

$$\Delta \left( \frac{1}{n^2} \right)_i = \sum_{j=1}^6 p_{ij} \varepsilon_j(r, \omega), \quad \varepsilon_{rr}(r) = \frac{\rho \omega^2}{8E} \left[ (3+\nu) R_2^2 - (1+3\nu) r^2 \right]$$

To isolate true relativistic time dilation from vibration and centrifugal runout, the HG-ODE engine deploys a dual-state continuous-discrete Extended Kalman Filter:

$$\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k} = \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1} + \mathbf{K}_k \left[ \mathbf{y}_k - \mathbf{h}(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1}, \mathbf{u}_k) \right]$$ $$\mathbf{h}(\mathbf{x}) = \begin{bmatrix} f_0 \left( 1 - \frac{\omega^2 (R_2^2 - R_1^2)}{2c^2} \right) + \mathbf{C}_{\text{strain}} \cdot \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}(\omega) \\ \mathbf{B}_{\text{hall}}(\delta x, \delta y) \end{bmatrix}$$

The mechanical envelope for spaceborne high-G centrifuges and re-entry testing is strictly governed by the maximum tensile hoop stress $\sigma_{\theta\theta} = \rho (\omega R_2)^2$ and the acoustic propagation velocity $c_s = \sqrt{E/\rho}$:

$$\sigma_{\text{hoop}} \le \frac{\sigma_{\text{yield}}}{\text{SF}}, \quad \Delta t_{\text{acoustic}} = \frac{R_2 - R_1}{c_s} \gg \frac{R_2 - R_1}{c}$$
